Slide 4 — Key Barriers & Opportunities
Four critical bottlenecks costing Breaking Games revenue — all solvable within 8 weeks
Barrier 1 Meta ads: highest traffic, lowest conversion (0.07%) Critical
Current state: Facebook drives 62,447 pageviews, 94.28% bounce, 0.07% conv. Meta spend = $37,201 (69% of total BG budget). Blended ROAS = 1.28x.
Root cause: Traffic-message mismatch. Ads attract broad audiences who are not purchase-ready. ~80% of Meta budget = TOF (Traffic), no MOF/BOF segmentation. Mobile UX compounds this.
Solution: Segment Meta into TOF 40% / MOF 30% / BOF 30%. Create Facebook-specific landing page. Run 3-step retargeting (Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7).
Target: Meta ROAS 1.28→2x over 60 days
Barrier 2 Mobile checkout: 98.7% abandonment — payment step failure Critical
Current state: 609 checkout sessions → 242 billing filled → 8 reached payment. The 242→8 drop is a payment interface failure, not buyer intent.
Root causes: Missing payment methods (Shop Pay, PayPal) · unexpected shipping cost reveal · mobile form UX failure · no visible guest checkout option.
Solution: Enable Shop Pay + PayPal Express. Remove surprise costs. Guest checkout prominent. Retarget 242 billing-address users. Set up Klaviyo cart flow at 1hr / 24hr / 72hr.
Impact: Est. +$1,123–$1,800 recovery / period, zero extra ad spend
Barrier 3Zero UTM tracking across all campaigns
No UTM parameters on Meta or Google. Every ROAS figure is estimated. Cannot confirm which campaigns drive which orders.
Fix (Day 1, 2–3 hrs): Add UTMs to all ad URLs. Connect BG Shopify to dedicated GA4. Install Meta Pixel with Purchase event.
Transforms all future analysis from estimated to exact
Barrier 4Sparkle*Kitty: 104 carts, $0 revenue
104 abandoned carts across two Sparkle*Kitty SKUs — most of any product — but $0 revenue in Shopify export. Possible causes: out-of-stock but listed, checkout bug on that SKU, or different SKU name in order history.
Action: Check Shopify product status. Test checkout manually. If checkout bug found — fix immediately, as it may explain part of the 98.7% abandonment rate.
Slide 5 — Strategic Recommendations
A three-phase approach can lift conversion and add meaningful revenue within 8 weeks
🔴 Phase 1 — Immediate (Week 1–2): Fix the leak
Rec 1: Add UTM tracking — Add UTM params to every Meta + Google ad URL. Connect BG Shopify to dedicated GA4. Install Meta Pixel with Purchase event.
Impact: real attribution. Zero cost. 2–3 hours. Do this first.
Rec 2: Fix mobile checkout UX — Enable Shop Pay + PayPal Express. Remove surprise shipping costs. Enable guest checkout. Test payment step on mobile.
Impact: est. +$1,123–$1,800 / period. Abandonment 98.7%→<90%.
🟡 Phase 2 — Short-term (Week 2–4): Recover lost revenue
Rec 3: Retarget 242 billing-address users — Export → Meta Custom Audience. 3-step sequence: Day 1 (product reminder + social proof) → Day 3 (urgency) → Day 7 (10% offer). Klaviyo cart flow at 1hr / 24hr / 72hr.
Impact: est. $261–$783 recovery. Minimal cost.
Rec 4: Investigate Sparkle*Kitty anomaly — Audit Shopify status. Test checkout manually. Cross-reference SKU names.
Impact: may uncover hidden revenue or a checkout bug affecting all products.
🟢 Phase 3 — Long-term (Month 2–3): Optimise for growth
Rec 5: Meta funnel segmentation — TOF 40% (awareness) · MOF 30% (product page visitors, gameplay creative) · BOF 30% (ATC + billing list, urgency + offer).
ROAS 1.28→2x over 60 days.
Rec 6: Dwellings bundle strategy — "Dwellings Complete Bundle" (Standard + LEGENDARY) at 10% discount. "Customers Also Bought" upsell. Post-purchase email sequence.
AOV $21.60→$30+ = +38% ROAS equivalent.
Rec 7: Optimise PMAX — Add negative keywords, upload Shopify product feed, add customer match list. Target conv. rate 0.85%→1.5%+ before scaling budget.
ROAS 1.34→1.6x+.
