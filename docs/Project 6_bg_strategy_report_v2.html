Root Cause Hypotheses
H1
Meta audience misalignment — High CTR + 0.43% purchase conv. + 67.1% Facebook bounce = audience is browsing, not buying. TOF campaigns are ~80% of Meta budget. No MOF/BOF segmentation in place.
H2
Mobile checkout UX is broken — 66% mobile traffic, 98.7% cart abandonment. 242 users filled billing addresses but only 8 reached payment — a payment step technical or UX failure. Industry avg is 85–90%.
H3
No retargeting infrastructure — 242 high-intent users never recovered. Meta pixel likely misconfigured or absent. No confirmed Klaviyo abandoned cart email flow in data.
H4
PMAX below conversion benchmark — 0.85% conv. rate vs Google's 1–2% ecommerce average. Drives strong awareness (2.6M impressions) but underperforms on conversions. Needs product feed + negative keywords before scaling.
H5
Low AOV limits ROAS ceiling — $21.60 avg AOV means even a 3x ROAS requires only $7.20 CPA. With PMAX CPC at $0.65, margin is structurally thin. Bundle strategy is required to improve revenue per order.
Role 1: Digital Strategy Consultant   5 actionable recommendations with funnel stage, e-commerce benchmarks, KPIs, and self-critique. All data is BG-only.
1
Fix Mobile Checkout UX ConversionCritical
Why: 98.7% abandonment vs industry avg 85–90% (Baymard Institute 2024). The 242→8 drop from billing to payment is a payment step technical failure, not intent. Shopify's Shop Pay improves mobile conversion by up to 50%.
For Breaking Games: Audit Shopify mobile checkout — enable Shop Pay / PayPal Express, ensure guest checkout is prominent, check for unexpected shipping cost reveal, reduce form fields. The billing→payment gap (242→8) points to a specific failure at the payment method step.
Benchmarks: MVMT Watches +18% mobile conv. via one-page checkout. Blenders Eyewear +30% completion reducing form fields 14→8.
📊 KPI 1: Checkout completion rate (target >5% from <1.5%)  |  📊 KPI 2: Mobile conv. rate (target >1.5%)
2
Meta Retargeting — 242 Billing-Address Users RetentionHigh ROI
Why: Billing-address completers are the highest-intent segment. Meta retargeting for cart abandoners converts 2–4× vs cold audiences (WordStream 2023). Klaviyo cart emails recover 3–5% of carts on average.
For Breaking Games: Export 242 users from Shopify → upload as Meta Custom Audience. Run 3-ad sequence: Day 1 (product reminder), Day 3 (urgency), Day 7 (10% offer). Set up Klaviyo abandoned cart flow at 1hr / 24hr / 72hr. Est. recovery: 242 × 5% × $21.60 = ~$261 min; at 15% = ~$783.
📊 KPI 1: Retargeting ROAS (target >3x)  |  📊 KPI 2: Cart recovery rate (target >5%)
3
Optimise PMAX Before Scaling ConversionData-backed
Why: PMAX conv. rate 0.85% is below Google's 1–2% ecommerce benchmark. The campaign has a high optimisation score (80.65) but that is Google's self-assessment — not an ROI indicator. 2.6M impressions show strong reach; the issue is conversion quality.
For Breaking Games: Add negative keyword lists (block non-gaming terms). Upload Shopify product feed directly to PMAX asset groups. Add customer match list from Shopify purchasers as a campaign signal. Ensure product images meet Google quality thresholds (>1200px). These technical fixes typically improve PMAX conv. rate by 0.5–1.5pp within 4–6 weeks.
📊 KPI 1: PMAX conv. rate (target >1.5% from 0.85%)  |  📊 KPI 2: PMAX ROAS (target >1.6x from 1.34x)
4
Meta Funnel Segmentation (TOF / MOF / BOF) ConsiderationMedium Effort
Why: ~80% of Meta budget is TOF (Traffic). Facebook conv. rate 0.43% vs Bing 4.1% confirms audience is browsing not buying. A three-layer funnel with distinct creative per stage is standard DTC practice for improving ROAS.
For Breaking Games: TOF (40%): Keep DA | Traffic TOF for Dwellings awareness. MOF (30%): Video viewers + product page visitors 30–90 days — gameplay/unboxing creative. BOF (30%): ATC users 7 days + billing-address list — urgency + offer creative. Requires separate creative assets for each stage.
📊 KPI 1: Meta purchase conv. rate (target >1% from 0.43%)  |  📊 KPI 2: Meta ROAS (target >2x from 1.28x)
5
AOV Uplift: Dwellings Bundle Strategy ConversionRevenue Multiplier
Why: $21.60 avg AOV makes profitability structurally difficult. Dwellings buyers naturally purchase multiple SKUs. A 50% AOV increase to ~$32 improves blended ROAS equivalent from 0.89x to ~1.34x without extra ad spend. Shopify bundle apps deliver 10–25% AOV uplift on average.
For Breaking Games: Create "Dwellings Complete Bundle" (Standard + LEGENDARY Kit) at 10% bundle discount. Add "Customers Also Bought" on product pages and cart. Post-purchase email sequence: "Complete your Dwellings experience" targeting single-SKU buyers with upgrade kit offers.
📊 KPI 1: AOV (target >$30 from $21.60)  |  📊 KPI 2: Units per transaction (target >2)

Role 2: Performance Marketing Analyst   Focused on measurable, data-driven optimisations with highest confidence and fastest feedback loops. All data is BG-only.

Priority 1 — Fix UTM Tracking (Data Foundation) Analyst

Zero attribution exists. Every cross-platform finding in this report is correlation-based. Without UTM parameters, it is impossible to confirm which campaigns drive which orders.
Action (2–3 hours, Day 1): Add UTM parameters to all Meta ad destination URLs:
?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=DA_Sales&utm_content={{ad.name}}
Connect Breaking Games Shopify store to a dedicated GA4 property. Install Meta Pixel with Purchase event + Shopify integration. This single step transforms every future analysis from correlation to real attribution.
Impact: Enables exact ROAS per campaign, per ad, per audience — currently estimated for all Meta campaigns.

Priority 2 — Fix Mobile Checkout (98.7% Abandonment) Analyst

The data is unambiguous: 609 checkouts started, 242 filled billing, only 8 reached payment. The 242→8 drop is a payment step failure, not a buyer intent problem. This is the highest-confidence, highest-impact fix in the entire dataset.
Action: Test the checkout flow manually on mobile. Check: payment method availability, SSL/trust badge visibility, unexpected cost reveals, page load speed at payment step. Enable Shop Pay and PayPal Express as one-tap alternatives.
Math: If abandonment drops from 98.7% to 90% (industry avg), that is ~52 additional completed checkouts × $21.60 AOV = ~$1,123 additional revenue per equivalent traffic period — with zero additional ad spend.

Priority 3 — PMAX Needs Technical Optimisation Analyst

PMAX - Breaking Games: 0.85% conv. rate, optimisation score 80.65. Google's ecommerce benchmark for PMAX is 1–2%. The campaign is at bottom quartile. The high score is Google's self-assessment — it does not reflect ROI performance.
Technical fixes (no budget increase needed): Upload Shopify product feed to PMAX asset groups. Add negative keyword list (block irrelevant terms). Add customer match list from Shopify order history. Ensure product images ≥1200px. These changes typically improve conv. rate by 0.5–1.5pp within 4–6 weeks.

Priority 4 — Retarget 242 Billing-Address Users Analyst

242 users completed the billing address step — the most intent-rich segment in the funnel. They are not being retargeted. This is a confirmed missed revenue opportunity.
Action: Export 242 users from Shopify → upload as Meta Custom Audience. Create a 3-step retargeting sequence (Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7). Simultaneously set up Klaviyo abandoned cart email flow. Cost: minimal (retargeting audiences are cheap to reach).
Projection: 242 users × 10% recovery × $21.60 AOV = ~$523 recovery. At 15% = ~$783. Low spend, high intent = strong ROAS expected.

Priority 5 — Investigate Sparkle*Kitty Zero Revenue Anomaly Analyst

Sparkle*Kitty: 104 abandoned carts (most of any product) but $0 revenue in Shopify export. Possible causes: (a) product out of stock but still listed, (b) checkout error on that specific product, (c) revenue tracked under a different SKU name, (d) product removed after cart period.
Action: Check Shopify product status. Test checkout manually. 104 high-intent cart additions with zero revenue = either a significant hidden revenue stream or a data integrity issue requiring immediate resolution.
